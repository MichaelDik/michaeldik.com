My Learning Principles

  • Deep Work - Focused time away from distractions, no email, slack, checking phone, etc
  • Inventing the Future - The best way to predict the future is to invent it, i.e. don't be worried about AI taking your job, go and continuously learn about how to use AI at the frontier of knowledge.
  • Aim to be good, not great - greatness is being good every day, a collection of good habits, heroic efforts have their time and place, but are largely unneeded. Good efforts daily compound.
